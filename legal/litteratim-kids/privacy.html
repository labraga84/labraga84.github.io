Privacy Policy

Android app: Litteratim Kids
Developer: LABraga Games
Last updated: 2026-11-25

Litteratim Kids is a word game for children aged 5 to 13, made by LABraga Games, to learn to write through play. This page explains what data the game uses. In short: we (LABraga Games) do not collect personal data, there are no accounts or ads, and nothing is shared. Only the parents' purchase uses a third-party service (see "Purchases").

WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

We do not receive any data. The game does not ask for photos, email addresses or accounts, has no ads, and does not use advertising or analytics services.

For the parents' purchase, the game uses Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity Technologies), which connects the game to Google Play. According to Unity, this service collects, for purchases to work and for operational analytics: device identifiers, purchase history, approximate location (country/region), diagnostics and crash reports. This data is encrypted in transit and is not shared with other companies. See Unity's privacy policy.

WHAT IS STORED ON THE DEVICE

For the game to work, the following is stored only on the device itself:

  • child profiles: the name or nickname typed by the parents, the character, the age group and the game preferences (board size, font and colours);
  • the game in progress and the move history, to continue later;
  • each profile's statistics and records;
  • in-game coins, the items obtained in the in-game shop and the number of games started each day (for the daily limit chosen by the parents);
  • the data for the parents' report: game scores, words played, mini-game answers and attempted words that do not exist;
  • the parents' area code and the full version purchase.

This data never leaves the device. It can be deleted by clearing the app's data in the Android settings or by uninstalling the game. In the game between children, guest names are only used for that game and are not stored.

INTERNET

No internet connection is needed to play: the dictionary is inside the game. When online, the game only connects to Google Play (and Unity's purchasing service) to show the price and confirm the purchase. The parents' area has links to this page and to the Terms of Use, which open in the browser.

PARENTS' AREA

The parents' area is protected by a 4-digit code, chosen by an adult the first time it is opened. The code is stored only on the device and is only used to enter the parents' area.

PURCHASES

The in-game shop only uses coins earned by playing, never money. The only real-money purchase (the full version, a one-time payment) is only in the parents' area and is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store any payment data; Google's Privacy Policy applies.

CHILDREN

The game is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families policies. We do not store any children's data. Technical purchase data is handled by Unity and Google under their own policies.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
